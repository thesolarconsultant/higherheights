The studio

The difference between footage and a film is the decision behind every single frame.

Higher Heights is a film and photography studio working across fashion, luxury, automotive and sport — on set with brands, agencies and the occasional very particular private client.

Every project starts the same way: find the frame that actually says something, then build the story around it. Everything else is logistics.

Studio

One pair of eyes
on the whole thing.

Higher Heights is run by Morgan — a filmmaker and photographer who shoots, directs and edits the majority of the work himself. That means the idea that gets signed off is the idea that gets shot, and the person cutting it was in the room when it was conceived.

The studio moves between brand films, commercials, product, aerial and event coverage. What stays constant is a preference for restraint: fewer setups, better chosen, and a cut that trusts the viewer.

“If it doesn't earn its place in the cut, it doesn't go in.”

01 Brief & references A conversation, a deck, a mood film — whatever exists. We agree what the film has to do before anyone talks about lenses.
02 Pre-production Treatment, shot list, schedule, locations, permissions. The unglamorous part that decides whether the shoot day runs.
03 Shoot Lean crew, deliberately. Enough hands to move fast, few enough that the room stays quiet and the subject stays comfortable.
04 Post & delivery Edit, grade, sound, captions. Master plus every ratio and cutdown the campaign actually needs.
