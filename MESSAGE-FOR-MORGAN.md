## On the jobs

For each of these — **Nexus, Oakridge, Matchroom, WatchTrader, RM69, NOBL,
WHOOP, Aduna, Spens Motors** — three or four lines is plenty:

- What did they actually need? What was the problem they came to you with?
- What was the constraint — budget, time, access, weather, one shot at it?
- What did you make — how long, how many cutdowns, where did it run?
- Anything that went right or badly wrong that's worth telling?

That last one matters more than it sounds. "We had forty minutes with the car
before the light went" is the kind of thing that makes a buyer trust you.
"Delivered a cinematic brand film" means nothing.

If some of these are under NDA or you'd rather not name them, say which and
I'll write round it.

## On how you actually work

- What does a typical job cost? Bands are fine — under £5k, £5–10k, £10–20k,
  £20k+. Pages with honest pricing convert better and filter out the people who
  were never going to book.
- How long from shoot to first cut to final?
- What's included as standard — usage rights, revisions, cutdowns, stills,
  music licensing?
- What do people most often get wrong when they brief you?
- What's the job you turn down?

## On the drone side

- Are you CAA certified? Which category — A2 CofC, GVC?
- What's the insurance cover?
- Anything you can't fly — airports, crowds, central London?

## On London

- Happy to travel, or London-focused?
- Call-out minimum?
- Any areas or venues you know particularly well?

## And the bigger one — you

This part isn't for the website. It's because the next thing worth building is
your personal brand, and that only works if it's actually you rather than a
version of you we invented.

- Why did you start doing this? Not the polished answer — the real one.
- What do you want this to look like in three years? More work, bigger work,
  different work, less work?
- What kind of job makes you want to get up at 4am for it?
- What's the thing you're better at than most people doing this?
- What do you want to be known for? If someone described you in one line to
  another person, what would you want them to say?
- Who do you actually want to work with — the dream client, the dream brief?
- What are you bored of being asked to do?
- Are you comfortable being on camera yourself, or would you rather stay behind
  it? (No wrong answer — it changes the approach completely.)
- What do you think of other people in this space? Anyone you rate, anyone whose
  stuff you think is all surface?
- What would you never put your name to?
