Corporate video production

Corporate video that people actually watch

Films for companies that need to explain something, sell something or hire someone — made the way a brand film is made, not the way a corporate video usually is. UK based, filming across the country and abroad.

What companies commission most

  1. Recruitment & culture films
  2. Company and brand stories
  3. Product and service explainers
  4. Case studies & testimonials
  5. Conference & event coverage
  6. Social cutdowns from one shoot

How it tends to go

Most corporate video fails for the same reason: it is written to say everything, so it says nothing. The fix is not a bigger crew or a longer film — it is deciding, before anyone picks up a camera, what one thing the film is for and who it is talking to.

A typical job is one or two shoot days, a small crew, and a cut that respects how long anyone will actually watch. You get the main film plus the cutdowns the campaign needs, from the same shoot, rather than paying twice.

Recent work includes a recruitment film for Nexus, property films for Oakridge Property Group, brand work for Aduna and WHOOP, and ongoing product film for WatchTrader & Co.

What you get

  1. A director who shoots and edits
  2. One or two days, not a fortnight
  3. Main film plus social cutdowns
  4. Stills from the same shoot
  5. Aerial built in where it earns its place
  6. Usage that covers paid media
